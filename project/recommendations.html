Recommendations

Not sure what to watch next? Start with these picks, grouped by what you like.

Best for beginners

Death Note

A single-season mystery with a clear story that is easy to follow. A great first anime if you like thrillers.

Demon Slayer

Beautiful animation and a simple, easy-to-follow story about a boy who becomes a demon hunter.

Haikyu!!

A friendly sports story about teamwork and growing together. No background knowledge needed.

Popular genres

Shonen

Action and adventure stories aimed at teens, full of friendship and training. Examples: Naruto and One Piece.

Romance

Stories about love and relationships, from school life to grown-up drama. Example: Toradora!

Isekai

A character is transported to another world, often a fantasy one. Example: Re:Zero.

Slice of Life

Calm stories about everyday life, friendship, and small moments. Example: Barakamon.

Educational anime

Cells at Work!

Explains how the human body works by turning blood cells and germs into characters.

Dr. Stone

Rebuilds civilization from scratch using real science and engineering ideas.

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Its alchemy follows strict rules, and the story raises thoughtful questions about science and ethics.
